Lens Types

Choose the lens that fits your eyes

Your prescription from the checkup decides the power — this guide helps you choose the lens type.

Single Vision

One power across the whole lens — corrects either distance vision or reading vision. Scratch-resistant and the most common choice for everyday glasses.

Best for: One clear job — seeing far away, or reading up close.

NPR 1,500
Enquire on WhatsApp

Bifocal

Two powers in one lens — the top for distance, a visible segment at the bottom for reading. Near and far in a single pair, no swapping glasses.

Best for: Adults who need help with both distance and reading.

NPR 3,500
Enquire on WhatsApp

Progressive / Varifocal

All distances in one seamless lens — far, middle (computer) and near blend gradually with no visible line. Our range: Progressive Varifocal NPR 6,500 and the all-distance Progressive Lens Pack NPR 8,500.

Best for: All-day wearers who want one pair for everything, with a natural look.

NPR 6,500 – 8,500
Enquire on WhatsApp

Photochromic

Clear indoors, darkens automatically in sunlight — one pair that works as glasses and sunglasses, with UV protection outdoors.

Best for: People moving between indoors and outdoors all day — riders, walkers, field work.

NPR 4,500
Enquire on WhatsApp

Blue Light Blocker

Lenses that filter blue light from screens, with an anti-reflective coating — made for long hours on phones, laptops and office computers.

Best for: Students, office workers and anyone with heavy daily screen time.

NPR 2,500
Enquire on WhatsApp

High Index Thin

Ultra-thin lenses for stronger prescriptions — your glasses stay light and slim instead of thick and heavy at the edges.

Best for: High-power prescriptions where regular lenses would look and feel bulky.

NPR 5,500
Enquire on WhatsApp

Polarized Sunglasses

Polarized lenses cut glare from roads, water and bright surfaces for sharper, more comfortable outdoor vision — UV400 protection. Our Polarized Sport is a customer favourite.

Best for: Driving, riding, travel and bright sunny days.
