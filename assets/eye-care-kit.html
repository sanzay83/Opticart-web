Care Guide

Look after your glasses the right way

How to clean your lenses

  • 1Rinse first. Run lukewarm water over the lenses to wash off dust — dry wiping grinds dust into the coating.
  • 2Spray lens solution. Use proper lens cleaner (like the Cleaning Kit Pro) — a drop on each lens is enough.
  • 3Wipe with microfiber only. Small, gentle circles with a clean microfiber cloth. Wash the cloth regularly so it doesn't trap grit.
  • 4Never use tissue, newspaper or your shirt. Paper fibres and rough fabric scratch lenses and strip anti-reflective coatings.

How to store your glasses

  • 1Always in the case. When they're not on your face, they belong in a hard case — pockets and bags are where frames bend and lenses scratch.
  • 2Face-up on surfaces. Rest glasses with the lenses facing up, never lens-down on a table.
  • 3Two hands on, two hands off. Taking glasses off with one hand twists the frame out of alignment over time.
  • 4Keep away from heat. Don't leave glasses on a sunny dashboard — heat can warp frames and damage coatings.

Contact lens care basics

  • 1Wash and dry your hands before touching lenses, every single time.
  • 2Fresh solution only. Store lenses in fresh solution in a clean case — never reuse old solution, and never use water.
  • 3Don't sleep in your lenses unless your doctor has specifically said you can — sleeping in lenses raises infection risk.
  • 4Replace on schedule. Follow the replacement cycle for your lens type, and replace the case regularly too.

New to contact lenses? We guide you on fitting and care in store — visit during store hours or ask on WhatsApp.

Do's & don'ts

  • ✓Do clean lenses daily and keep a microfiber cloth with you.
  • ✓Do bring loose or bent frames to us for adjustment instead of forcing them.
  • ✓Do get your eyes re-checked every year, or sooner if your vision changes.
  • ✕Don't wipe dry, dusty lenses — rinse or spray first.
  • ✕Don't wear glasses on top of your head — it stretches the frame.
  • ✕Don't share contact lenses, and never wear a torn or expired lens.
